# Privacy Notice

**Project:** Sakura Tunes  
**Package:** com.sakuratunes.music  
**Maintainer:** xboyrex

This notice explains privacy considerations users should review before using Sakura Tunes. It is not a claim that the application is completely offline or that no information is transmitted.

## 1. Application and network behavior

Sakura Tunes is an Android client that can connect to external services for streaming, search, artwork, lyrics, translations, music recognition, scrobbling, synchronized listening, updates, or casting. The exact network requests depend on the application version, enabled features, selected providers, and user actions.

When a feature communicates with an external provider, that provider may receive request data such as network address, device or client metadata, search terms, requested media identifiers, or account-related information required by that service.

## 2. Optional integrations

Features such as Last.fm scrobbling, Listen Together, music recognition, lyrics providers, Google Cast, and account synchronization may involve third-party systems. Their data practices are governed by the relevant provider's privacy policy and settings.

Review provider permissions and account settings before enabling an integration. Disable optional integrations that you do not intend to use.

## 3. Credentials and account information

Do not share passwords, session tokens, API keys, or account recovery codes in public issues or support messages. Where an integration requires authentication, use the application's intended sign-in flow and review the provider's account security controls.

## 4. Local data and permissions

The application may store user preferences, playback state, library information, cached artwork, or downloaded/cache data locally so that features can operate. The exact data retained can change between versions. Review Android app permissions and storage controls on your device.

## 5. Children's privacy

The project is not specifically designed as a service for children. A parent or guardian should review the application and any connected third-party service before allowing a child to use it.

## 6. Third-party links and services

Links, APIs, artwork, media metadata, and other resources supplied by external services are outside the maintainer's control. Their availability and privacy practices may change independently of Sakura Tunes.
